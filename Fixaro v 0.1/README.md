# Fixaro 🛠️

> **Fast, simple, and low-friction home services platform for Argentina.**

Fixaro flips the traditional model: instead of searching for professionals, users post their problem and let nearby professionals come to them with fast responses. The product is built on simplicity, speed, and a mobile-first philosophy.

## 🚀 Core Concept

**"User posts a problem → professionals respond quickly → issue gets resolved"**

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Quick Posting**: Post a problem using text, a photo, and location data.
- **Smart Diagnostic**: Automated category detection, possible causes, estimated price range, and time (based on predefined data, not complex AI).
- **Active Professionals**: Service providers browse available jobs and send quick responses.
- **Direct Chat**: Built-in chat between the client and the professional.
- **Urgent Mode**: Priority notifications sent to professionals for emergency requests.
- **Trust System**: Ratings, reviews, and identity verification to build a reliable community.
- **Professional Catalog** *(Secondary Feature)*: Optional browsing for users who prefer to search.

## 🧠 Product Philosophy

- **No complex flows** or heavy forms.
- **Fast interaction** is the priority.
- **Mobile-first experience** mirroring WhatsApp-like communication.
- **Focus on solving problems**, not just browsing profiles.
- The "wow" factor comes from speed and perceived intelligence, not from heavy AI or over-engineering.

## 🎯 Target Market

- **Location**: Argentina.
- **Audience**: Users with low trust in online payments who prefer simple UX.
- **Market**: The informal home service market.

## 💻 Tech Stack (Suggested)

- **Backend**: Laravel
- **Frontend**: Blade or React (Mobile-first)
- **Database**: MySQL
- **Hosting**: Shared hosting (Early stage)

## 🗺️ Development Stages

### 1. MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
- Post a problem
- Professional responses
- Direct chat
- Basic diagnostic tools

### 2. Validation
- Acquire real users
- Measure response times
- Validate problem-solution fit

### 3. Growth
- Improve the trust and rating system
- Better matching algorithms
- Enhanced notifications

### 4. Monetization
- Credits per contact for professionals
- Featured professional profiles

## 🎯 Goals

1. **Reduce Friction**: Make hiring home services as easy as sending a WhatsApp message.
2. **Speed**: Achieve fast responses (ideally under 10 minutes).
3. **Build Trust**: Bring reliability to a highly fragmented market.

---

*Note: The project should remain simple at first. Avoid overengineering to maintain focus on speed and usability.*
